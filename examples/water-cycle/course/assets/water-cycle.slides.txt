[1] The Water Cycle | A short introduction | Where does a puddle go? | 1
[2] Four stages, in a loop | The cycle has four stages, in order: | evaporation, condensation, precipitation, collection. | 2
[3] 1. Evaporation | Liquid water turns into water vapour. | It is driven by heat from the sun. | 3
[4] 2. Condensation | Water vapour cools and turns into tiny droplets. | The droplets form clouds. | 4
[5] 3. Precipitation | Water falls from clouds as rain, snow, | sleet or hail. | 5
[6] 4. Collection | Water gathers in oceans, lakes, rivers | and underground. | 6
[7] Plants join in: transpiration | Plants release water vapour through their leaves. | Evaporation and transpiration together are called | evapotranspiration. | 7
[8] Where is Earth’s water? | About 97% of Earth’s water is salt water | in the oceans. | 8
[9] Where does fallen water go? | Runoff: water flowing over land into streams and rivers. | Infiltration: water soaking into the ground and | becoming groundwater. | 9
[10] Ice can skip a step | Sublimation: ice turns directly into vapour, | without becoming liquid first. | 10
