<a id="q1"></a>
**Q1 (Hard).** Which sequence lists the four stages of the water cycle in order?

- A. Evaporation, then condensation, then precipitation, then collection, after which the cycle begins again
- B. Evaporation, then precipitation, then condensation, then collection, after which the cycle begins again
- C. Condensation, then evaporation, then collection, then precipitation, after which the cycle begins again
- D. Collection, then precipitation, then evaporation, then condensation, after which the cycle begins again

<a id="q2"></a>
**Q2 (Hard).** Which statement about where Earth's water is found matches the lecture?

- A. About 3% of Earth's water is salt water in the oceans, and the rest is fresh water in lakes
- B. About 97% of Earth's water is salt water in the oceans, and the rest is fresh water, ice and vapour
- C. About 97% of Earth's water is fresh water in lakes, and the rest is salt water in the oceans
- D. About 50% of Earth's water is salt water in the oceans, and the rest is fresh water and ice

<a id="q3"></a>
**Q3 (Medium).** A forest releases moisture into the air on a warm morning. Which pairing of term and meaning is correct?

- A. Condensation: plants release water vapour through their leaves, and with evaporation it is called evapotranspiration
- B. Transpiration: ice turns directly into vapour, and with evaporation it is called evapotranspiration
- C. Precipitation: plants release water vapour through their leaves, and with evaporation it is called evapotranspiration
- D. Transpiration: plants release water vapour through their leaves, and with evaporation it is called evapotranspiration

---

<a id="q4"></a>
**Q4 (Hard).** Rain is falling from a grey cloud onto a lake. Which pairing of the two stages involved is correct?

- A. Precipitation is water gathering in oceans, lakes, rivers and underground; collection is water falling from clouds as rain, snow, sleet or hail
- B. Precipitation is water turning into water vapour by the sun's heat; collection is water falling from clouds as rain, snow, sleet or hail
- C. Precipitation is water cooling into tiny droplets that form clouds; collection is water gathering in oceans, lakes, rivers and underground
- D. Precipitation is water falling from clouds as rain, snow, sleet or hail; collection is water gathering in oceans, lakes, rivers and underground

---

<a id="q5"></a>
**Q5 (Hard).** Snow on a cold, dry mountain slope disappears without ever forming a pool of liquid water. Which term fits?

- A. Condensation, in which water vapour cools and turns into tiny droplets that form clouds
- B. Infiltration, in which water soaks into the ground and becomes groundwater
- C. Sublimation, in which ice turns directly into vapour without becoming liquid first
- D. Collection, in which water gathers in oceans, lakes, rivers and underground

---

<a id="q6"></a>
**Q6 (Medium).** Warm air from a hot shower meets a cold bathroom mirror and fine droplets appear on the glass. Which stage does this match?

- A. Evaporation, in which liquid water turns into water vapour, driven by heat from the sun
- B. Condensation, in which water vapour cools and turns into tiny droplets that form clouds
- C. Precipitation, in which water falls from clouds as rain, snow, sleet or hail
- D. Collection, in which water gathers in oceans, lakes, rivers and underground
