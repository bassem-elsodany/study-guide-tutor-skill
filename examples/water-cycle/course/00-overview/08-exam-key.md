<a id="q1"></a>
**Q1: A.** The order is evaporation, condensation, precipitation, collection.
- B: Swaps condensation and precipitation; clouds must form before water falls.
- C: Starts with condensation and puts collection before precipitation.
- D: Starts at collection, which is the last stage.
- Hint: water has to rise before it can fall.

<a id="q2"></a>
**Q2: B.** About 97% of Earth's water is salt water in the oceans.
- A: Swaps the percentage: salt water is the large share, not 3%.
- C: Calls the large share fresh water, but it is salt water.
- D: 50% is not the figure the lecture gives.
- Hint: most of the planet's water is in the oceans.

<a id="q3"></a>
**Q3: D.** Transpiration is plants releasing water vapour through their leaves; with evaporation it is evapotranspiration.
- A: Condensation is vapour turning into droplets, not a plant process.
- B: Ice turning directly into vapour is sublimation.
- C: Precipitation is water falling from clouds.
- Hint: think of leaves.

<a id="q4"></a>
**Q4: D.** Precipitation is water falling from clouds as rain, snow, sleet or hail; collection is water gathering in oceans, lakes, rivers and underground.
- A: Swaps the two stages.
- B: The sun's heat turning water into vapour is evaporation.
- C: Cooling into tiny droplets is condensation.
- Hint: one is falling, the other is gathering.

<a id="q5"></a>
**Q5: C.** Sublimation: ice turns directly into vapour without becoming liquid first.
- A: Condensation needs vapour to cool into droplets, which is the opposite direction.
- B: Infiltration is water soaking into the ground.
- D: Collection is water gathering in oceans, lakes, rivers and underground.
- Hint: no liquid stage.

<a id="q6"></a>
**Q6: B.** Condensation: water vapour cools and turns into tiny droplets that form clouds (here the cold mirror plays the part of the cold air).
- A: Evaporation goes the other way, from liquid to vapour.
- C: Precipitation is water falling from clouds, not droplets forming on glass.
- D: Collection is where fallen water gathers.
- Hint: warm vapour meets something cold.
