A small course the tutor made from `assets/water-cycle.pdf`, a 10-slide deck. It is here so you can see exactly what you get.

## Start here

**Open the practice page:** [00-overview/09-study-player.html](00-overview/09-study-player.html) (download it and double-click; it works offline in your browser). It opens on the lessons and also has a quiz with instant feedback, flashcards that come back at the right time, a timed exam, your progress and a place for your own highlights and notes.

## The chapters

| # | Chapter | Slides | What you learn |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [The four stages](chapter-01-the-four-stages/01-lesson.md) | 2-6 | evaporation, condensation, precipitation, collection |
| 2 | [Where the water goes](chapter-02-where-the-water-goes/01-lesson.md) | 7-10 | plants, runoff, soaking in, salt water, ice |

Read a chapter's lesson first (the short "At a glance" block, then the story), then practise it in the player (or read it in the player's Lesson tab, which shows the same text). The files in each chapter folder are the quiz, its answer key, the flashcards and a list of every fact with its slide number.
